# Badge artwork templates

The SVG templates in this folder (`roundel.svg`, `shield.svg`, `hexagon.svg`, `ribbon.svg`) were
drawn for OpenCredential from basic shapes. They are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so institutions can
reuse and rebrand them without attribution.

`$primary`, `$secondary`, `$text` and `$label` are filled in by
`opencredential.authoring.templates.render()`; the result is sanitised like any upload.
